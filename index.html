Honest Verdict

⚽ Manly United Tour — Verdict: Well Paced

Munich (4 nights), Prague (2 nights) and the Austrian Alps (4 nights) are all well-sized stays. The tour is structured around football, so even the shorter Prague leg is purposeful. Luke will barely notice the pace.

🏙️ Milan Base Camp — Verdict: Smart Call

Four nights in one place is still the right move after 12 days of moving around. Early train from Kirchberg on the 25th, arrive Milan midday, unpack once and don't touch the bags again until the evening flight to Paris. Two full city days covers Milan's highlights — San Siro, the Duomo, the Last Supper, the Navigli. Then Lake Como as a day trip. Clean, relaxed, and genuinely the best way to do northern Italy.

🏰 Paris + Disneyland — Verdict: Well Planned

The 3-night Paris stay with a clear day-by-day structure (sightseeing → Disney → Disney) is one of the best-paced legs of the whole trip. The Monday Disneyland day with lower crowds is a real win.

🇬🇧 London — Verdict: Tight but Intentional

One night in London is genuinely tight — but the trade-off is 3 nights of quality family time in Kent, which is the right call. The Spurs tour + Westminster covers the London essentials. Gatwick from Kent on departure day is also far easier than from central London.

🌟 Overall — Verdict: Well Structured

8 destinations in 25 days is a big trip, and the structure holds up beautifully. No single leg is rushed. The Manly United Tour is well-paced throughout. The Family Extension is anchored by four nights in Milan — the smartest single decision in the itinerary — with Como as a day trip, before an evening flight to Paris, two Disneyland days and a warm family finish in Kent. This is a trip the Milligans will talk about for years.
