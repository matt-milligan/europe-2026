Honest Verdict

⚽ MUFC Tour — Verdict: Well Paced

Munich (4 nights), Prague (2 nights) and the Austrian Alps (4 nights) are all well-sized stays. The tour is structured around football, so even the shorter Prague leg is purposeful. Luke will barely notice the pace.

🇮🇹 Early Italy — Verdict: Well Paced

Two nights in Verona is a great reset after the tournament. Arrive via the Brenner Pass on the evening of the 24th, then a full free day on the 25th to explore at your own pace. No rushing, no packing twice in a row. The optional Mantua day trip on Day 2 gives even more flexibility.

🏖️ Como + Milan — Verdict: Just Right

Two nights each is the sweet spot for both. Lake Como's weekday timing (Mon–Tue) means lighter crowds on the ferries. Milan is compact enough that 2 focused days covers the highlights comfortably.

🏰 Paris + Disneyland — Verdict: Well Planned

The 3-night Paris stay with a clear day-by-day structure (sightseeing → Disney → Disney) is one of the best-paced legs of the whole trip. The Monday Disneyland day with lower crowds is a real win.

🇬🇧 London — Verdict: Tight but Intentional

One night in London is genuinely tight — but the trade-off is 3 nights of quality family time in Kent, which is the right call. The Spurs tour + Westminster covers the London essentials. Gatwick from Kent on departure day is also far easier than from central London.

🌟 Overall — Verdict: Well Structured

9 destinations in 25 days is a big trip, but the structure holds up beautifully. No single leg is rushed. The MUFC Tour is well-paced throughout. The Family Extension builds from a relaxed Verona opening, through the scenic Lake Como and buzzy Milan, into the purposeful Paris/Disneyland stay and a warm family finish in Kent. This is a trip the Milligans will talk about for years.
